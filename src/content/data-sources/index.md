## Estimates back series

The past estimates that underpin the projections are based on the ONS Mid-Year Population Estimates (MYE) up to mid-2024. These include the revisions made to the estimates by ONS in July 2026. The GLA modifies these estimates before using them in the projections.  

Local authority level estimates:

- for the periods 2001-02 to 2020-21 international migration flows and population are remodelled to provide an annual series in which annual population change is fully accounted for by the standard components of population change (births, deaths, and migration), removing the 'unattributable population change' pseudo-component included in the official ONS series.

- estimates are further adjusted to account for identified issues in official estimates of the population of children. These adjustments are modelled using data on the number of pupils present in schools, captured in the annual schools census, and numbers of patients registered with GP surgeries.

Ward and MSOA level estimates:

- published estimates from ONS are rescaled to be consistent with the adjusted local authority estimates series.

- annual net migration for each area is estimated as the residual of annual population change, births and deaths.

- synthetic in and out migration flows for each area, consistent with the net flows are modelled.

Annual estimates of births, deaths and internal migration are used unchanged from official data.

## Other inputs

Other inputs include:

- Projected age-specific mortality rates for England, taken from the ONS 2024-based National Population Projections.

- Births by age of mother for local authorities and MSOA areas (published as ad-hoc releases by ONS).

- 2021 census estimates of: dwellings, population by residence type, household representative persons.

- Annual numbers of homes by Lower-layer Super Output Area (LSOA) published by the Valuation Office.

- Net changes in housing stock by local authority, published by MHCLG

- Assumed future net change in dwellings by LSOA in London produced as part of the 2026 London Strategic Housing Land Availability Assessment.
