The GLA Demography team produces a range of demographic projections to support planning and policy across London. This report provides an overview of the 2024-based population projections, published in August 2026. 

The 2024-based projections are the first to incorporate data from the new London SHLAA – the first major update to the housing assumptions used since 2017, as well as a number of changes and improvements to the methodologies used within the models.

This release includes:

- Trend-based population projections for local authority districts and regions in England and Wales to 2051.

- Housing-led population projections for electoral wards, Middle-layer Super Output Areas (MSOA), and local authorities in London to 2051.

Outputs are available: 

- As Excel workbooks with population by single year of age and sex, plus summary components of change

- As machine-readable files for use in analysis

- via the interactive Population Projection Explorer

## Variants

Multiple variants, based on alternative scenarios of future fertility, mortality, and migration, are produced as part of the projection process. This document will provide an overview of the ‘core’ set of variants (central, high, and low) that are intended to reflect a reasonable range of possible future outcomes for use in common planning or policy applications. 

The demographic assumptions of the core projections are: 

- **low:** low fertility, low life expectancy, migration based on an average of patterns from the previous 10-years 

- **central:** central fertility, central life expectancy, migration based on an average of patterns from the previous 13-years 

- **high:** high fertility, high life expectancy, migration based on an average of patterns from the previous 15-years 

The housing-led projections all incorporate the same assumptions about future housing delivery in London - derived from the 2026 London Strategic Housing Land Availability Assessment - but vary in terms of the assumed level of housing delivery in surrounding districts. 

For districts outside of London, the central assumption about future housing delivery was derived as follows: 

- Objectively assessed housing need for each district was calculated using the National Planning Policy Framework's *Standard Method*

- Assumed housing delivery for each district was calculated by multiplying its Standard Method need figure by the ratio of: *London's assumed annual housing delivery* / *London's Standard Method need figure*

- For the low variant housing delivery outside of London is assumed to be 15 percent higher than this, for the high variant it is assumed to be 15 percent lower

***Note:** assumptions about housing delivery for areas outside of London are included in the model as a mechanism for adjusting the relative shares of the wider region’s population within and outside of London.  These projections were produced without reference to current or future local plans or assessments of housing capacity in areas outside of London and in no way represent a view of likely future housing delivery in those areas. Where results for areas outside of London are included in the housing-led projections, it is for the purpose of providing transparency about the results for London.*
